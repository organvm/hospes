# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in HOSPES a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, relationship boundaries, and unpublished work.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, sustained disruption,
publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a
professional setting is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject,
or temporarily or permanently exclude contributions or participants whose behavior violates this
code. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
