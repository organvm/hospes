# Pilot Brief 01 — Prior Professional Guest

**Status:** RESEARCHING  
**Relationship class:** C2 (prior professional relationship — Unlicensed Therapy appearance)  
**Relationship owner:** Ari  
**Route:** Producer email to prior booking address  
**City / Studio:** Los Angeles — Ari's Melrose studio or equivalent LA room  
**Social cost:** 2 / 5 — warm re-engagement, not a cold ask  

---

## Guest Archetype

A comedian who previously appeared on *Unlicensed Therapy*. Single prior episode, strong personal
disclosure in that conversation, well-regarded in the LA stand-up scene. Has been on other
interview-format podcasts since. Not a household name nationally; strong regional reputation and a
loyal existing audience. Ari knows this person; the ask is a natural follow-up, not a favor.

**Why this person:** An alumna of the prior show is the lowest-social-cost way to open a new
format. The relationship is already established; the outreach is a re-engagement, not a pitch.
The comedian register will also help calibrate how funny the format can get without becoming a
comedy podcast.

**Why now:** A returning guest creates an implicit narrative — "last time we talked about X, now we
want to go further with both of us in the room." That frame makes the invitation easier to accept
and sets up a natural arc for the episode.

---

## The Claim

*The second conversation is always more honest than the first.*

A comedian who has already opened up on one mic carries a different relationship to disclosure on
the second appearance. The prior episode is a document. Coming back means being willing to be
measured against it — or to revise it. That tension is the engine of the conversation.

---

## Stress Test Plan

- **Host chemistry check:** Can Ari and Anthony co-host without one of them disappearing? Does the
  comedian guest create a triangle or a lecture? The stress test is whether the conversation feels
  like three people talking, not two hosts interviewing one subject.
- **Interruption protocol:** How do both hosts handle a moment when they disagree with each other
  in front of the guest? One planned point of divergence will be introduced naturally; observe
  whether it generates energy or confusion.
- **Depth floor:** Can the format go somewhere genuinely uncomfortable without losing the guest?
  The prior Unlicensed Therapy episode sets a baseline. This conversation should reach a depth the
  first one didn't.

---

## Intended Artifact

**Clip package:** One two-minute moment of genuine disagreement between all three people — guest
included — that works without setup. Extracted in post; no context card needed. This tests whether
the format produces a shareable unit that sells the show without explaining it.

---

## Rotating Segment

**Claim / Stress Test / Artifact** (fixed) +  
**Rotating:** *The Revisit* — pull one specific statement from the guest's prior Unlicensed Therapy
episode, read it back verbatim, ask whether they still believe it. Three minutes maximum. No
debate; just the honest check. This segment is pilotable because the archive exists and the guest
expects it.

---

## City + Studio Logistics

Los Angeles. Ari's Melrose studio is the default. If Melrose is unavailable, a similar
conversation-room setup in Silver Lake or Los Feliz. No visible microphones; two redundant hidden
audio channels. Table and chairs only — no podcast desk. Guest arrives with no brief, no topic
list, no pre-interview.

---

## Relationship Route + Social Cost

Ari's producer emails the prior booking address with a direct note: "We're recording a new show,
I'd love you back, it's a different format." No publicist. No agent. No social ask. Social cost: 2.
If no response in 14 days, one follow-up, then move to next candidate.

---

## What This Pilot Must Prove

**Host chemistry.** Two hosts, one guest, one room — does it feel like a conversation or a
committee? By the end of this recording the team will know whether the three-person dynamic is the
right architecture for the show, or whether one host should carry most conversations while the
other plays a lighter role. That decision cannot be made in the abstract. This pilot makes it.
